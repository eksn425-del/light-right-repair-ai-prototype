# Public Paper Asset Manifest

This is the public-safe subset of the complete private package
`eksn425-del/soict-2026-paper-assets`. It is derived from frozen source tag
`soict-2026-write-ready-v1`, branch `research-repro-v1`, commit
`6b64fe43451e2e0330930a5677cbe3432307282b`, run ID
`baseline_20260909_clean`.

## Included

- Figures 1, 3, 4, 6, 7, 8 and 9 in PNG, PDF and SVG-container forms.
- Public-safe paper tables 2, 5, 6, 7, 8 and 9 as derived CSVs.
- Figure/table captions, bibliography, and submission checklist.

## Private-only assets

Figures 2 and 5, and Tables 1, 3 and 4, are omitted here because their
site-specific geometry or identifiers may expose the study area. Obtain them
from the private paper-assets repository only when authorized.

## Release hygiene

No original CAD/DXF/DWG/SKP/OBJ, sensor coordinate table, Radiance
intermediate, or unauthorized real raw data is included. The PDF/SVG figure
companions embed the final raster pixels in publication containers; they are
not claimed to be newly vectorized line art.

For per-asset source CSV/script/run ID, see `FIGURE_CAPTIONS.md` and
`TABLE_CAPTIONS.md`. For the complete checksum inventory, use the manifest in
the private paper-assets repository.
