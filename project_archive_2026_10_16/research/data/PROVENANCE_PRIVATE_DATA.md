# Private data provenance

The canonical site is derived from the Xieyan group deliverables and the Route B geometry-audit workflow. Source CAD, SketchUp and OBJ binaries are private project material. They are intentionally not treated as public training data and must not be uploaded to a public repository or external service without the team’s permission.

The clean run records hashes and paths in its stage metadata. The derived canonical CSV/GeoJSON/OBJ files are kept for local reproducibility, but the repository policy should remain private until the team confirms data rights.
