## Boundaries

No new solver experiment was run. Raw private geometry, receiver coordinates, HB models, solver logs and per-cell matrices were not independently reopened. Hashes prove byte correspondence, not truth, authenticity, chronology or as-built fidelity. The retained normalization-identity amendment does not establish the origin of the original incorrect hash. The paper must retain the narrow two-implementation speedup, same-site cohort limits, solar-vector difference, approximate geometry, non-volume-matched correction and restricted-reproducibility caveats. Citation additions have a separate primary-source review. Author confirmation of rights, authorship, AI-use disclosure and single-submission status is still needed.
