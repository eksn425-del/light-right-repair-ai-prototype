## Submission and anonymity

[Official guidelines](https://icores.scitevents.org/Guidelines.aspx): English anonymous PDF through PRIMORIS. Regular Papers require **10,000–50,000 non-whitespace characters**, including references, tables, graphs and appendices. Initial review length is character-based, not an 18-page cap. Accepted Full Papers have 25 proceedings pages; Short Papers 20; up to four paid extra pages may be allowed.

Remove author identities, identifying references and acknowledgments. Do not publicly post the submitted manuscript during review. Originality and no substantial overlap with published or simultaneously submitted work are required. The guideline explicitly requests citations to the AI system in sections using AI-generated text. Human authors remain responsible for accuracy and declarations.

## AI disclosure

[Official AI policy](https://icores.scitevents.org/AiTools.aspx): name tools, affected portions and purpose, including generated code/figures/text. Authors must verify original literature, cannot attribute authorship to AI, and cannot fabricate/manipulate scientific evidence. Translation and language assistance are permitted. Generated conceptual illustrations require labeling and tool/prompt disclosure. The page also restricts processing manuscripts under review through public AI platforms for peer-review-like tasks.

There remains an official wording conflict: disclosure is required in acknowledgments, while anonymous guidelines require removing acknowledgments; another sentence allows an appropriate section. An identity-free AI Use Disclosure with affected-section citations is a provisional handling, **not chair-approved**. Author/chair confirmation remains a submission gate. Do not suppress actual analysis/code/writing assistance or reclassify it as mere grammar correction.
