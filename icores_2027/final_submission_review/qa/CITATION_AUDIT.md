# Citation audit: integrated ICORES manuscript

Audit date: 19 October 2026. Scope: references already present in the recovered preference/solar source bundles and final/paper/references.bib; no new literature added. The preference bundle bibliography was available locally, so no private-repository fetch was necessary. Scientific verification used original publishers, proceedings PDFs and author-hosted originals. Search aggregators were discovery aids only and were not treated as authoritative evidence.

## Findings and recommended action

- No confirmed substantive title, author, publication-year or DOI correction was found in the cited scientific entries. Preserve the current careful distinctions between established methods and this case's new evidence.
- Two metadata conflicts must not be silently “fixed”: the original Ladybug proceedings PDF has **two authors**, whereas its current publisher landing metadata lists a third; the original ROR author-laboratory bibliography gives **415–435**, whereas some indexes give 416–436. Retain the manuscript's two-author Ladybug entry and 415–435 ROR pagination.
- The ACM landing pages for Nanongkai and Ward returned access errors. Their original texts verify identity, authors, year and scientific scope; the existing DOI strings are retained, but direct publisher DOI-metadata verification remains incomplete. This limitation is not a demonstrated citation error. Do not substitute Nanongkai's DOI with the conflicting secondary-index suffix 1920983.
- Several bibliography entries are presently uncited in the integrated manuscript (listed below). Keeping them in the .bib source is harmless; do not force them into prose merely to make the reference list longer. Standard BibTeX includes only cited works.
- OpenAI is an assistance disclosure, not a scientific source. Keep it separate in purpose from the scientific reference audit; do not invent the underlying model or version.

## Claim-level conclusion

The related-work paragraphs inspected in final/paper/manuscript.tex are appropriately bounded: rank acceptability is scenario-dependent, ROR is explicitly not implemented, representative regret is not presented as novel, relative regret is distinguished from absolute regret, and simulation-tool citations are not presented as case validation. Preserve these qualifications during condensation. No cited external work proves the new shortlist is globally minimax-optimal, architect-preferred, regulation-compliant or construction-ready.

The original literature has been checked at the depth needed for these background claims. This audit does not constitute author approval, systematic literature review, verification of every experiment in each source, or certification of unavailable publisher metadata.
