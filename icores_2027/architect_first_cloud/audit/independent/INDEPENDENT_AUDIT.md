## Required manuscript corrections/precision

1. **Two tie policies must not be conflated.** Production enumeration sorts score descending, then A descending, then H descending. It has no explicit final pair-key tie break; current scores have no exact ties, so this has no effect on present results. Production Top10 filters A>0 before head(10). Natural numeric pair ordering is the later statistics protocol for exact-K within-cohort comparisons. If adding a prospective total-order safeguard, label it as such rather than claiming it was historical code.
2. **N=0 adds +0.05, with zero rank influence.** The normalized zero-range value is zero, and the score uses 0.05(1−n_N). Saying N has no score contribution is false. Changing only its coefficient shifts every score equally when other coefficients remain fixed. Historic NEW_LOW_SENSITIVE also changes A/H, so any rank change does not demonstrate an effective N penalty.
3. **Seven Top10 are A/V-dominated, coherently.** The full preference score also rewards H; A/V front and score list answer different questions. Do not imply all ten are Pareto-efficient or that A/V-dominated means universally inferior. Top10/front overlap is C9/C24, C9/C15, C9/C13.
4. **Weights are declared modeling preferences, not architect-validated priorities.** No stakeholder elicitation, architect user study, decision-quality improvement, structural-cost model, or calibrated utility model was found. Preserve the fixed .45/.35/.15/.05 weights for historical reproduction. Do not tune to improve current results. A/V alternatives and transparent metrics support deliberation; they do not establish which trade-off an architect should choose. Normalized weights also depend on the declared universe ranges and cannot be transported unchanged without reconsideration.
5. **Review statuses are layered.** Historical rule-flat HB review is available for all Top10; translation evidence only for three. Neither solver agreement nor containment establishes ownership rights, legal eligibility, structural safety, design quality, or constructability. Do not relabel the shortlist as ten validated architectural designs.
6. **V is a nominal proxy.** Shared or overlapping footprints and bounding-box canonical geometry mean per-building summed removal differs from whole-scene union removal and translated removal. The correction is not volume-matched. It is not a demolition bill or cost estimate. V entries on 2.5D/HB comparison rows differ only by ≤9.09e-13 m³ rounding; they represent shared inputs, not independent volume validation.
7. **No 100% accuracy or global recall.** Coverage20 is same-site, outcome-blind coverage rather than probability sampling/independent-site validation. Constant N/shared V limit composite-score independence. MAE .45 m² averages a single 9m² discrepancy over20; it does not imply sub-grid precision. Top-K overlap is within the stated cohort. The legacy score-rank versus HB-area-rank rho .883399 compares different targets and must not be portrayed as inferior earlier physical accuracy.
8. **Retain corrected history and label its date.** Old C24 adverse rays were traced in historical local evidence to erroneous protruding upper walls. They are not a normal consequence/trade-off of pure subtraction. Four containment corrections plus six legacy replays are historical local runs; current cloud work is aggregate reanalysis. Earlier BLOCKED audit sections survive as historical checkpoints below superseding current resolutions and must not be treated as current missing evidence for those old runs.
9. **Model-conditional inference only.** Fixed winter-solstice samples, 3m grid, z=.1m, north=0 assumption, constant WEA carrier and nonidentical HB/pvlib rays limit inference. No new site model, measured weather, raw-field measurement, full630 HB run, new physical experiment or cross-city generalization has occurred here.
